What the app does

Everything you need, nothing that touches your store.

Read-only by design

The app reads orders, products and customers. It never creates, changes or deletes anything in your store.

Orders in minutes

New orders reach your dashboard within minutes; history loads in the background from the date you choose.

Ads and email next to revenue

Connect Google Ads, Meta, TikTok and Klaviyo to see spend, campaigns and blended ROAS beside your sales.

Several stores, one view

Add stores for each country or brand and see them separately or combined, in EUR, USD or GBP.

Private by default

Customer names, emails and addresses are not stored. Data is encrypted in the EU and deleted 30 days after you uninstall.

Billing in Shopify

Pick a plan in the app; it appears on your Shopify bill. 14-day free trial on paid plans, cancel any time.

What the app can access

When you install Delta Point, Shopify asks you to approve read-only access to:

  • Orders, including amounts, dates, status, products and discount codes
  • Products, inventory and locations
  • Customers (only the customer ID, order count and total spent are kept)
  • Fulfilments, returns, price rules and discounts
