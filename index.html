Frequently asked questions

Short answers about data, platforms and billing.

What is blended ROAS?

Blended ROAS (also called MER, marketing efficiency ratio) is your total store revenue divided by your total ad spend across all platforms. Unlike the ROAS each ad platform reports, it does not depend on platform attribution, so the same sale is never counted twice. Try it with your own numbers in the free ROAS & MER calculator.

Which platforms does Delta Point connect to?

Shopify for orders and revenue, Google Ads, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) Ads and TikTok Ads for spend and campaign results, and Klaviyo for email campaigns and flows.

How fresh is the data?

New Shopify orders appear within minutes. Advertising and email data refresh at least once a day, and recent days are re-read so late platform corrections are picked up.

Can I see several stores, countries and currencies together?

Yes. Each store and market can be viewed on its own or combined, and amounts can be shown in EUR, USD or GBP using daily European Central Bank exchange rates.

Is my data safe and GDPR compliant?

Access is read-only: Delta Point never changes your store or campaigns. Data is stored encrypted in the EU, the names, emails and addresses of your customers are not kept, and a Data Processing Agreement is included. Wave3 is an EU company based in Slovenia.

How does billing work?

Plans are chosen and paid in Shopify, with a 14-day free trial on paid plans. The charge appears on your Shopify bill and you can change or cancel it there at any time. Yearly billing gives one month free.
